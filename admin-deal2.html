  • To prevent fake IDs and scams, all admin deals will be conducted exclusively via WhatsApp groups instead of Messenger.
  • Either the buyer or seller will contact our official WhatsApp number to create a group and add the respective admin.
  • After adding the admin to the group, they must be made the Group Admin. The group creator must then resign their Admin Status so the group remains solely under the official admin's control.
  • The group name must be set according to the total deal amount and admin fee.
  • Admin Fee: 50 BDT for deals up to 500 BDT. 60 BDT for 500 to 1,000 BDT. Above 1,000 BDT, the fee increases by 20 BDT for every additional 1,000 BDT. (The fee will be informed before the deal starts).
  • Once the buyer sends the main deal amount and the admin fee to the admin, the admin's initial duty is complete. The funds will be safely held in escrow and will not be released to the seller until the buyer's confirmation is received.
  • The exchange of the ID will take place entirely in the private inbox of the buyer and seller. The admin will not check the ID, take responsibility for the ID's login or security, nor store any ID information.
  • After the seller provides the ID and necessary information in the inbox, the buyer will log in and verify it at their own responsibility.
  • Once the buyer successfully receives and verifies the ID in the inbox, they will Confirm the admin in the group. Upon receiving the buyer's confirmation, the admin will immediately send the main deal amount to the seller.
  • When the seller confirms receiving the money, the deal will be considered Completed/Closed. The admin will not accept any liability for account-related issues after the deal is closed.
  • Before closing the deal, the buyer must fully verify the ID and login details. No complaints will be entertained after the deal is closed.
