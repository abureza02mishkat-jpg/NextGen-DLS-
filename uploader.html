1
First, upload your logo using this tool and copy the generated link.
2
Open the Dream League Soccer (DLS) game and go to the "MY CLUB" section.
3
Tap on the "CUSTOMISE" option.
4
Tap on the "LOGO" button.
5
Paste the copied URL into the "ENTER LOGO URL" box.
6
Finally, tap on "CONFIRM" to successfully download and apply it.
